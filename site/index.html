Quick start

From download to app, in five steps

Allow about ten minutes the first time and about thirty seconds after that. You need a Mac on macOS 13 or later, an Apple ID (a free one is enough) and the app file for your device.

  1. 01

    Download and move to Applications

    Open Lodestar-arm64.dmg and drag Lodestar onto the Applications shortcut beside it.

    Download for Mac

  2. 02

    Open it the first time

    Release builds aren't notarized by Apple, so macOS stops the first launch. Open Lodestar once, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. On macOS 13 and 14 you can right-click the app and choose Open instead. Or clear the flag in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Lodestar.app

    When macOS asks whether Lodestar may find devices on your local network, choose Allow. Apple TV discovery needs it.

  3. 03

    Sign in with your Apple ID

    Open Apple ID in the sidebar, enter your email and password, then the six-digit code Apple sends to your other devices. Tick Remember me for one-click refreshes; the password is kept in your Mac's Keychain. A separate Apple ID just for sideloading is a sensible choice.

  4. 04

    Connect a device

    iPhone or iPad: plug it in, unlock it and tap Trust. On iOS 16 and later, turn on Developer Mode in Settings › Privacy & Security.

    Apple TV 4K: keep it on the same Wi-Fi as your Mac. One already paired with this Mac appears by itself. To pair a new one, open Settings › Remotes and Devices › Remote App and Devices on the TV, then choose Pair in Lodestar's Devices page and type the code. First-time pairing hasn't been verified on hardware yet, so please report how it goes.

  5. 05

    Install, then refresh within 7 days

    Drop the .ipa (or .tipa for Apple TV) into the Install page, pick the device and press Install. On an iPhone, trust your Apple ID once in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.

    Signatures from a free account last 7 days. My Apps shows the countdown; Refresh re-signs an app in one click.

Privacy

No telemetry, no update check, no analytics

Lodestar talks to three kinds of server, and only to do the job you asked for.

  • Apple

    Sign-in, certificates, App IDs and provisioning profiles. Sign-in uses Apple's SRP protocol, so your password is never sent as plain text.

  • An anisette server

    Apple requires device-identity headers on these requests. The default community server sees your IP address and the provisioning exchange, never your password. You can run your own.

  • Your devices

    Over USB or your local network.

Questions

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free and there is no paid tier, account or trial. If you'd like to support the work, there's a way to buy me a coffee below, with no obligation.

Why do apps stop working after 7 days?

That's Apple's rule for apps signed with a free developer account. Lodestar counts down each app and re-signs it in one click before it expires. A paid Apple developer account extends this, but Lodestar doesn't need one.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Notarization needs a paid Apple Developer ID, and release builds aren't notarized yet. The app is the same code you can read and build from GitHub. Step 02 of the quick start shows how to open it.

Which devices work?

Installing to an iPhone over USB and to an Apple TV 4K already paired with your Mac are verified on hardware. iPad, Wi-Fi sync for iPhones and first-time Apple TV pairing are implemented but not yet verified; the README keeps the current list.

Does it run on Intel Macs or Windows?

Release builds are for Apple silicon. Intel Macs are expected to work if you build from source but haven't been tested. There is no Windows or Linux version.

Is it affiliated with Apple?

No. Lodestar is an independent project. Install only software you're entitled to run, and follow Apple's developer terms.
