# Lodestar 1.1.0

Lodestar now has a website, a disk image download, and a way to say thanks.

<img src="../images/support-light.jpg" alt="The note in Lodestar saying the app is free, with Buy me a coffee and Not now buttons" width="520">

## Highlights

- **Download the disk image.** Open `Lodestar-arm64.dmg` and drag Lodestar to
  Applications. The zip is still attached for anyone who prefers it, and
  `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists both checksums.
- **A website**, with a quick start, screenshots and working copies of the app's
  controls: [asroya.github.io/lodestar](https://asroya.github.io/lodestar/).
- **Lodestar is still free.** After your first successful install (or, if you already
  have apps installed, your first refresh), a note says so once,
  with an optional *Buy me a coffee* link. Every feature stays free and there's no
  obligation. The note never appears again and never covers a sign-in or pairing
  prompt; the link stays at the bottom of the sidebar. Choosing it opens the website's
  support section in your browser. Lodestar itself sends nothing.

## Opening it the first time

Release builds aren't notarized. On macOS 15 and later, open Lodestar once, then choose
**Open Anyway** in System Settings › Privacy & Security. On macOS 13 and 14, right-click
the app and choose **Open**. Or run:

```bash
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Lodestar.app
```
